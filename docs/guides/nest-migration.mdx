People leave Nest Aware for two reasons: the subscription price keeps climbing, and every minute of footage lives in Google's cloud. Nightjar flips both — recording stays on your disk, detection runs on your hardware, and the optional cloud plan costs about half as much.

This guide is honest about the tradeoffs. What you keep: 24/7 recording, smart alerts (person, vehicle, animal, package), remote live view, and event history. What you lose: the Nest app and Google Home integration. Your existing Nest cameras can now come along via an experimental bridge — with caveats — or be replaced with RTSP hardware, which remains the robust path.

<Warning>
**Nest cameras do not expose RTSP**, so they cannot connect to Nightjar directly. An **experimental bridge** using Google's Smart Device Management (SDM) API is now available: the node hands your Nest stream to go2rtc's native `nest:` source, and the camera then records and detects like any other. Setup requires your own Google Cloud project and a one-time $5 Device Access registration — see [Adding cameras → Nest cameras](/guides/cameras#nest-cameras-experimental-sdm-bridge). Know the limits: it needs 2021-or-newer cams (or older ones with SDM RTSP), battery cameras cannot stream continuously, and video still transits Google's servers. For anything you want rock-solid, the hardware swap below is still the recommended migration.
</Warning>

## The plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Inventory your Nest devices">
    List every camera and doorbell, where it is mounted, and whether it is wired or battery. Note which ones matter most — you can migrate in stages. Battery cameras are the hardest to replace one-for-one, because continuous RTSP streaming needs continuous power.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stand up a Nightjar node">
    Follow the [quickstart](/quickstart). You need a Linux box (NUC, Pi 5, NAS) and a disk. Do this first, before buying cameras — it is free, and you can verify the node works on your network with a single cheap camera.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick replacement cameras">
    Any camera with RTSP works. Solid, widely available picks by Nest device role:

    | Nest device | Replacement class | Example |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | Nest Cam (outdoor, wired) | 4K/2K PoE bullet or turret with RTSP | Reolink RLC-810A class |
    | Nest Cam (battery) | Wired PoE or plug-in Wi-Fi camera — battery cams generally cannot stream RTSP continuously | Reolink RLC-810A (PoE) or a plug-in Wi-Fi model |
    | Nest Doorbell | RTSP-capable video doorbell | Reolink Video Doorbell, Amcrest AD410 |
    | Nest Cam (indoor) | Any 2K indoor RTSP camera | Reolink E1 Pro class |
    | Nest Cam with floodlight | PoE floodlight camera with RTSP | Reolink floodlight series |

    **PoE vs Wi-Fi, honestly:** PoE is more reliable (one cable for power and data, no Wi-Fi dropouts) but you have to run Ethernet and probably buy a small PoE switch. Wi-Fi cameras are easier to place but still need a power outlet, and a wall of Wi-Fi cameras will congest a home network. If you are drilling holes anyway, run the cable.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run both systems in parallel for a week">
    Keep Nest Aware active while the new cameras come online in Nightjar. Compare coverage, night vision, and alert quality. Fix camera angles and motion problems while you still have a fallback. Your Nest cameras keep working in the Google Home app the whole time — the two systems do not interact.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export your Nest history before canceling">
    Once your subscription ends, your Nest video history is gone. Export it first via [Google Takeout](https://takeout.google.com) (select the Nest product). You can also download individual clips from the Google Home app. Do this **before** canceling — history is deleted per Google's retention policy after the subscription lapses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cancel Nest Aware">
    Cancel in your Google account subscriptions. The cameras themselves keep offering live view in Google Home without a subscription, so you can keep a couple around as spares, sell them, or connect them to Nightjar through the experimental SDM bridge.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The cost math

Monthly subscription, side by side (prices as of the current Nest lineup):

| | Nest Aware | Nest Aware Plus | Nightjar Free | Nightjar Plus | Nightjar Pro |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Price | $10/mo | $20/mo | $0 | $4.99/mo | $9.99/mo |
| Event history | 30 days | 60 days | 3 days (cloud) | 30 days | 60 days |
| 24/7 recording | Cloud, Plus only | Google's cloud | Your disk | Your disk | Your disk |
| Smart alerts | Included | Included | Free, on your hardware | Included | Included |

Nightjar has an up-front hardware cost that Nest does not. A realistic example for a three-camera home currently on Nest Aware Plus ($240/yr):

| One-time hardware | Approximate street price |
| --- | --- |
| 3 × PoE camera (Reolink RLC-810A class) | ~$210 |
| Raspberry Pi 5 kit or used mini-PC | ~$100–150 |
| 1 TB disk + small PoE switch | ~$100 |
| **Total** | **~$410–460** |

Switching to Nightjar Plus ($60/yr) saves about $180/yr versus Aware Plus, so the hardware pays for itself in roughly **2 to 2.5 years** — faster if the free tier's 3-day cloud history is enough for you (then you save the full $240/yr and break even in under 2 years), and faster still versus a household with add-on cameras or future price increases. After payback, the savings recur; the cameras are yours either way.

<Note>
Hardware prices are approximate street prices, not quotes. The point of the math is the shape: a one-time cost that replaces a subscription that never ends.
</Note>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I keep using the Nest app?">
    Yes, for your Nest cameras. Nest hardware keeps working in the Google Home app with or without Nightjar running — the systems are independent. Nightjar cameras are viewed in the Nightjar local UI or at [app.nightjar.ca](https://app.nightjar.ca). There is no way to see Nightjar cameras in the Google Home app.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens to my old Nest clips?">
    They live in Google's cloud and are deleted after your Nest Aware retention window — and your access ends when the subscription does. Export what you want to keep via Google Takeout before canceling. Nightjar cannot import Nest history; the export is for your own archive.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will the Nest bridge support my camera model?">
    The experimental bridge uses Google's Smart Device Management API, which covers most modern Nest cameras that work in the Google Home app: 2021-and-newer models stream via WebRTC, and some older ones expose an RTSP live stream through SDM. Battery cameras that sleep cannot stream continuously, so they are a poor fit for 24/7 recording. It requires a one-time $5 Device Access registration with Google. Setup and per-model notes: [Adding cameras → Nest cameras](/guides/cameras#nest-cameras-experimental-sdm-bridge); rough edges are tracked in [issue #7](https://github.com/Snapsonic/nightjar/issues/7).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I lose remote access by leaving Nest?">
    No. Remote live view is free on every Nightjar plan, including Free, with zero port forwarding. See [Remote access](/guides/remote-access).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
